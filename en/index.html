Experience

What I built in my current job, on top of the day-to-day work on pick-ups, delivery notes and documents in SAP.

Penguin Random House

Transportation & Logistics Analyst · Apr 2026 – present

  • Pick-up orders dashboard in Power BI

    Power BI · DAX · Power Query

    Traceability for every pick-up order: pending orders, critical status by each carrier's SLA, ageing, purge reasons and a customer drill-through. Used across the operations area and presented to sales management.

    A replica on fictional data is under Projects.

  • A database that refreshes itself

    Excel · automated cross-checks

    I automated the daily refresh of the pick-up orders database with Excel cross-checks, so the dashboard and the alerts always work with today's data.

  • Daily SLA alerts to carriers

    Power Automate · Power BI · Excel

    Every day, the pick-up orders that reach the deadline agreed with their carrier are flagged, and each carrier gets an email with only its new ones: about 7 a day. Before, the only notice was the weekly list of every pending order, sent on Wednesdays, which still goes out.

    Carriers hear about a missed deadline the same day, not up to a week later.

  • SLA penalties backed by data

    Power BI · alerts · internal reporting

    With the record from the alerts and the dashboard, we agreed penalties for carriers that miss the SLA, except for exceptions reviewed case by case.

    The regional carrier's average delivery time dropped from about 22 to 18 days.

  • Pick-up Power App for Market Self Final rollout

    Power Apps · forms · automatic PDF

    Merchandisers, who stock the stores and generate the returns, used to request pick-ups by email, and the information arrived unclear. I built a Power App where they enter the pick-up and the store details: the request reaches our inbox automatically and the pick-up order PDF is generated, ready to send to the carrier. Market Self is a company that recently joined the group.

    Each order used to take 4–5 minutes to prepare; now it's automatic, at 6 to 10 orders a day. Failed pick-ups and back-and-forth with the carrier went down.

  • Monthly KPI report for Market Self

    SAP · charts · monthly review

    At the start of every month I build the performance deck from SAP data: delivery notes, packages by zone, billed volume and other KPIs. I present it with my area manager in the monthly meeting with the team in Chile.

This work uses company information, so there are no screenshots, code or real data here. The pick-up orders project rebuilds the dashboard on invented data.

03

How I work

  1. Clear rules before automating

    I agree on statuses, deadlines and exceptions with the people who run the process. Only then do I automate, and every rule is written down.

  2. One number for everyone

    Rules live in one place and are tested, so the dashboard, the alerts and the reports never contradict each other.

  3. AI as a tool

    I design the flow, the rules and the controls; I write the code with AI assistance (Claude Code), reviewing and testing every part.
